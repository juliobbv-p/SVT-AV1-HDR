How the savings accumulate

Saving against the baseline after each commit, in percent of the baseline. Instruction counts are exact; the cycle curve is the lowest of several runs per commit and still wanders by a few tenths of a point from one commit to the next.

Where the savings come from

Instruction savings (percentage points of the baseline, mean of the two clips), summed over the commits of each primary area and of each nature. Instruction counts understate pure SIMD work, which mostly shortens the time per instruction.
